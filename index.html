Professional Profile

Clinical practice, medical consulting, education, and service.

Dr. Curry is a North Vancouver family physician practicing comprehensive, relationship-based primary care at Kal'númet Primary Care Clinic. His clinical background also includes full-service family practice in Ontario, Indigenous interprofessional primary care, virtual rapid addiction medicine, and remote diabetes consultation for Indigenous communities.

His medical consulting work includes independent medical file review and IME services. He brings a family-medicine perspective to complex records, with an emphasis on impartiality, evidence, function, and reports that answer the referring party's questions directly.

He is a Clinical Instructor with the UBC Department of Family Practice and serves on the BC College of Family Physicians Indigenous Physician Committee and the Doctors of BC Insurance Committee. His MSc training and peer-reviewed neuroscience research continue to inform a careful, evidence-oriented approach to clinical and professional work.
