How a written opinion is organized

  1. Referral questions

    Restated as received, so each answer can be matched to its question.

  2. Records reviewed

    An inventory of the material provided, with relevant gaps noted.

  3. Clinical chronology

    Key events, investigations, and treatment set out in date order.

  4. Analysis

    Material evidence weighed, with assumptions and limits stated plainly.

  5. Opinion

    A direct answer to each referral question.

Proportionate
Attention follows the referral question and the material evidence.
Independent
Conclusions are separated from assumptions, advocacy, and unsupported inference.
Readable
Reports are organized for practical use by clinical and non-clinical readers.
Timely
