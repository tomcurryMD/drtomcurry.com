Core service

Independent Medical Review

Careful record synthesis, evidence-based reasoning, and direct answers to the questions that matter to the referring organization.

Asynchronous file review

Review of medical records and referral materials with a clear clinical chronology, identification of material evidence, and a focused written opinion. Well suited to questions that do not require an in-person examination.

Medical advisory work

Independent analysis of diagnosis, investigations, treatment, prognosis, function, restrictions, recovery barriers, and return-to-work considerations for insurers, employers, legal professionals, and public organizations.

Selected IME services

Independent medical examinations are considered where a direct assessment adds value. Scope, jurisdiction, records, referral questions, timelines, conflicts, and fees are confirmed before an assignment is accepted.

Proportionate Attention follows the referral question and the material evidence.
Independent Conclusions are separated from assumptions, advocacy, and unsupported inference.
Readable Reports are organized for practical use by clinical and non-clinical readers.
Timely Asynchronous work allows focused turnaround without avoidable scheduling delay.

Relevant experience

Clinical breadth applied to medical review

A generalist perspective is useful when a file crosses diagnostic categories, contains competing explanations, or must be understood in the context of the whole person and the demands of work.

Dr. Curry's review and advisory experience includes work involving large provincial workers' compensation systems, independent assessment organizations, and occupational-health settings. Client and organization identities are kept confidential unless disclosure is authorized.

His clinical background includes comprehensive longitudinal family medicine, Indigenous primary care, virtual rapid addiction medicine, chronic-disease consultation, prenatal care, care of older adults, office procedures, and care for patients with complex medical and social circumstances.

Assignments are considered across Canadian jurisdictions where licensing and the nature of the work permit. Every engagement remains subject to conflict screening, an agreed scope, appropriate records, and a secure method of information transfer.

Professional Profile

Clinical practice, medical consulting, education, research, and service.

Dr. Curry is a North Vancouver family physician practicing comprehensive, relationship-based primary care at Kal'númet Primary Care Clinic. His prior work includes full-service family practice in Ontario, Indigenous interprofessional primary care, virtual rapid addiction medicine, and remote diabetes consultation for Indigenous communities.

He is a Clinical Instructor with the UBC Department of Family Practice and serves on the BC College of Family Physicians Indigenous Physician Committee and the Doctors of BC Insurance Committee.

His MSc training and peer-reviewed neuroscience research inform a disciplined, evidence-oriented approach to clinical and professional work.
